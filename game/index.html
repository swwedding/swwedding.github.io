📜 Murder in the Township — Rules

Setup

  • Use one standard 52-card deck.
  • Separate into Suspects (A/J/Q/K), Weapons (black 3–10), Motives (red 3–10), and Locations (the 2s).
  • Draw one card from each type face-down — this is the hidden solution.
  • Shuffle the remaining 48 cards and deal equally to all players.

Card Types

  • Suspects — All Aces, Jacks, Queens, Kings (16 cards).
  • Weapons — Black 3–10. The number = exact word length of the weapon. ♣ Misfortune/Accident · ♠ Hate/Violence
  • Motives — Red 3–10. The number = exact word length of the motive. ♦ Greed/Money · ♥ Love/Passion
  • Locations — 2♣ Forest · 2♠ Workshop · 2♦ Jeweler · 2♥ Home

Round Structure

  • On your turn, choose one other player and ask about one card type.
  • They must show you one matching card if they have any.
  • If they have multiple matches, they choose which to reveal.
  • If they have none, they say so. Play passes clockwise.

Question Rules

  • Ask about only one card type per turn.
  • Revealed cards are shown privately — only you see which one.
  • Others may note a card was shown, not which card.
  • Log everything in the Question Tracker tab.

Accusation Rules

  • At any time on your turn, make a formal accusation.
  • State the Suspect, Weapon, Motive, and Location.
  • Reveal the four solution cards. Correct? You win. Wrong? You're eliminated.
  • Use the Word Builder to verify weapon/motive word lengths.
